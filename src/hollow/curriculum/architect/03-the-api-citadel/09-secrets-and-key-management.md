# Secrets and Key Management

> **Why this matters for you:** Leaked credentials are among the commonest incidents; managing them is part of owning a service.

## You should be able to
- Use a secrets manager, rotation and least privilege
- Explain envelope encryption with KMS
- Keep secrets out of logs, images and repos

## Exam questions
- How do you rotate a database password without downtime?
- What is envelope encryption, and why use it?
- A secret was pushed to Git. What do you do in the next hour?
