# Delivery Guarantees

> **Why this matters for you:** At-most, at-least and exactly once decide every messaging design; seniors know which one they really have.

## You should be able to
- Explain each guarantee and where it breaks
- Use acknowledgements, publisher confirms and persistence in RabbitMQ correctly
- Explain why exactly-once delivery needs idempotency

## Exam questions
- Your consumer crashes after the database write but before the ack. What happens?
- What do publisher confirms protect you from?
- Can a system have exactly-once delivery? Defend your answer.
