# Backpressure and Flow Control

> **Why this matters for you:** A slow consumer with no backpressure becomes a full broker and an outage.

## You should be able to
- Use prefetch, queue limits and consumer scaling
- Explain backpressure end to end
- Choose between dropping, buffering and slowing producers

## Exam questions
- What does RabbitMQ prefetch do, and how do you pick a value?
- The queue grows by 10k messages a minute. What do you check first?
- When is dropping messages the right answer?
