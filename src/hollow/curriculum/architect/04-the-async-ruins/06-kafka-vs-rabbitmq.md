# Kafka vs RabbitMQ

> **Why this matters for you:** You know RabbitMQ well; knowing when a log beats a broker is the senior addition.

## You should be able to
- Explain logs vs queues: partitions, offsets, consumer groups, retention
- Explain ordering and replay in each
- Choose between them for a given workload

## Exam questions
- Survey events must be replayed to rebuild analytics. RabbitMQ or Kafka?
- How does Kafka keep ordering, and what breaks it?
- What does a consumer group rebalance do to your service?
