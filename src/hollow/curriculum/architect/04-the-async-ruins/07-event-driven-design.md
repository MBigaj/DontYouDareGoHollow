# Event-Driven Design

> **Why this matters for you:** Events decouple teams but bring schema evolution and eventual consistency with them.

## You should be able to
- Distinguish events, commands and queries
- Design event schemas and evolve them safely
- Choose choreography or orchestration

## Exam questions
- Event vs command: give one of each from your platform.
- How do you add a field to an event without breaking consumers?
- Choreography got hard to follow. When do you switch to orchestration?
