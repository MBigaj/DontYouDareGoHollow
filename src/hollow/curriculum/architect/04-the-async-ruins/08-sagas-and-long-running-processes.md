# Sagas and Long-Running Processes

> **Why this matters for you:** Multi-step business processes across services need sagas, compensation and timeouts.

## You should be able to
- Design orchestrated and choreographed sagas
- Write compensating actions
- Handle timeouts and partial failure in long processes

## Exam questions
- Order, payment, shipping: design the saga and its compensations.
- What if a compensation itself fails?
- Saga vs two-phase commit: why do most systems pick sagas?
