# Structured Logging and Correlation

> **Why this matters for you:** Logs are evidence in incidents only if they are structured and correlated.

## You should be able to
- Log structured events with request and trace ids
- Decide what to log, at what level, and what never to log
- Query logs during an incident

## Exam questions
- What belongs in every log line of a request?
- How do you connect a log line to its trace?
- Where do logs leak personal data, and how do you stop it?
