# Alerting on Symptoms

> **Why this matters for you:** Paging on causes creates noise; burn-rate alerts on SLOs page only when users suffer.

## You should be able to
- Alert on symptoms, not causes
- Build multi-window burn-rate alerts
- Cut alert fatigue

## Exam questions
- CPU at 90% for 10 minutes: page or not?
- Explain a multi-window, multi-burn-rate alert.
- Half the team ignores alerts. What do you change?
