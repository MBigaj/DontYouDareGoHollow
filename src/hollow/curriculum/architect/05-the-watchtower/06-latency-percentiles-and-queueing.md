# Latency, Percentiles and Queueing

> **Why this matters for you:** Tail latency and queueing theory explain why systems fall over at 80% utilisation.

## You should be able to
- Reason with p50, p95, p99 and why averages lie
- Explain tail latency amplification in fan-out
- Apply Little's law and utilisation curves

## Exam questions
- A request fans out to 10 services with a 1% chance of being slow each. How often is it slow?
- Why does latency explode near full utilisation?
- Use Little's law to size a worker pool.
