# Load Testing and Capacity Planning

> **Why this matters for you:** Knowing your limits before users find them is how seniors plan scale.

## You should be able to
- Design load tests that resemble real traffic
- Find the bottleneck and the breaking point
- Turn results into capacity plans and autoscaling settings

## Exam questions
- How do you load test a RabbitMQ-backed pipeline?
- Your test shows 2,000 RPS max. What do you do with that number?
- Why do load tests in staging often lie?
