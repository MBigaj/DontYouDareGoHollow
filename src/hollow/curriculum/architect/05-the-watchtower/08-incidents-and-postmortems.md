# Incidents and Postmortems

> **Why this matters for you:** Leading an incident and writing a blameless postmortem are visible senior skills.

## You should be able to
- Run incident response: roles, communication, mitigation first
- Write a blameless postmortem with real action items
- Find contributing causes, not one root cause

## Exam questions
- You get paged: error rate 30%. Your first 10 minutes?
- What makes a postmortem action item good?
- Why do 'five whys' often stop too early?
