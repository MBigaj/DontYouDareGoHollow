# Terraform Fundamentals

> **Why this matters for you:** Infrastructure as code is expected of seniors who own services end to end.

## You should be able to
- Manage state, modules, workspaces and plan/apply safely
- Import and refactor existing infrastructure
- Review a Terraform plan for risk

## Exam questions
- Two people apply at once. What protects you?
- How do you rename a resource without destroying it?
- What do you look for when reviewing a plan?
