# Compute on AWS: Lambda, ECS and EKS

> **Why this matters for you:** You migrated Lambda to ECS; seniors can explain that choice against the alternatives and costs.

## You should be able to
- Compare Lambda, ECS Fargate and EKS on latency, cost and operations
- Explain cold starts and provisioned concurrency
- Choose compute for a new service and defend it

## Exam questions
- Why ECS over Lambda with provisioned concurrency for your migration?
- When would Lambda be the better choice again?
- What changes at 10x traffic for each option?
