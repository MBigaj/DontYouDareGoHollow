# Autoscaling

> **Why this matters for you:** Scaling on the wrong signal is slow or expensive; queue-driven services need different signals.

## You should be able to
- Configure horizontal pod and service autoscaling
- Scale workers on queue depth
- Avoid flapping and cold-start gaps

## Exam questions
- Why is CPU a bad scaling signal for an I/O-bound service?
- How would you autoscale RabbitMQ consumers?
- Traffic doubles in one minute. What does your scaling do?
