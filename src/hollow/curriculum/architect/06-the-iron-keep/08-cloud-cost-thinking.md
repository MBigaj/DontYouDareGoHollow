# Cloud Cost Thinking

> **Why this matters for you:** Seniors weigh cost as an architecture constraint, not an afterthought.

## You should be able to
- Find the main cost drivers of a service
- Use reserved, spot and right-sizing
- Put a monthly cost on a design

## Exam questions
- Your AWS bill jumped 40%. Where do you look first?
- What does cross-AZ data transfer cost a chatty service?
- Estimate the monthly cost of an ECS service with 6 tasks.
