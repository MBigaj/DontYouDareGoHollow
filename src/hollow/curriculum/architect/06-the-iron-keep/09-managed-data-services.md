# Managed Data Services

> **Why this matters for you:** RDS, ElastiCache, SQS and SNS trade control for operations; choosing well is architecture.

## You should be able to
- Compare RDS and Aurora, ElastiCache options, SQS and SNS
- Plan backups, failover and maintenance windows
- Know when managed services are the wrong choice

## Exam questions
- SQS vs RabbitMQ on Amazon MQ for a new service?
- What happens during an RDS Multi-AZ failover?
- When does Aurora pay for itself?
