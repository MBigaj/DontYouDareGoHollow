# TCP in Practice

> **Why this matters for you:** Timeouts, retransmits and connection reuse explain many 'random' latency spikes.

## You should be able to
- Explain the handshake, retransmission, flow and congestion control
- Explain keep-alive, connection reuse and TIME_WAIT
- Spot TCP-level causes of latency

## Exam questions
- Why does the first request to a service take longer?
- What is head-of-line blocking?
- A service runs out of ephemeral ports. How?
