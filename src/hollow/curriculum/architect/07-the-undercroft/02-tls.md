# TLS

> **Why this matters for you:** Every request pays for TLS; termination choices affect latency and security.

## You should be able to
- Explain the TLS 1.3 handshake and certificates
- Choose where to terminate TLS and re-encrypt
- Explain mTLS between services

## Exam questions
- What happens in a TLS 1.3 handshake?
- Terminate at the load balancer or the pod?
- When is mTLS worth it inside a VPC?
