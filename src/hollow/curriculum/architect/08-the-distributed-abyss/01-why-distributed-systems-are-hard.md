# Why Distributed Systems Are Hard

> **Why this matters for you:** Partial failure, unreliable networks and clocks are the root of most distributed bugs.

## You should be able to
- Explain partial failure and the eight fallacies
- Reason about what a caller knows after a timeout
- Spot distributed assumptions in single-machine code

## Exam questions
- A call times out. What do you actually know about the remote side?
- Name three fallacies of distributed computing you have seen bite.
- Why is 'just retry' not a strategy?
