# Consistency Models

> **Why this matters for you:** Linearizable, causal, eventual, read-your-writes: choosing the right one is the core of design fights.

## You should be able to
- Define the main consistency models with examples
- Choose per feature, not per system
- Explain the user-visible effects of each

## Exam questions
- Which parts of a social feed need strong consistency, and which can be eventual?
- What is read-your-writes, and how do you provide it with replicas?
- Linearizability vs serializability?
