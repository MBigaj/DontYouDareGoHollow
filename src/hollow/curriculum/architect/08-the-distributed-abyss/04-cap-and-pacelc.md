# CAP and PACELC

> **Why this matters for you:** CAP is often misquoted; PACELC is the version that helps in real designs.

## You should be able to
- State CAP correctly and its limits
- Use PACELC to reason about latency vs consistency
- Classify real databases

## Exam questions
- What does CAP actually say, and what does it not?
- Where do PostgreSQL, MongoDB and DynamoDB sit in PACELC?
- During a partition, would you choose availability for payments?
