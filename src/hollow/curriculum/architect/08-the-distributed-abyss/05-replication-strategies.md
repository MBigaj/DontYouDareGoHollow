# Replication Strategies

> **Why this matters for you:** Leader, multi-leader and leaderless replication trade consistency, availability and latency.

## You should be able to
- Compare single-leader, multi-leader and leaderless replication
- Explain quorums (R + W > N) and their limits
- Explain conflict resolution

## Exam questions
- When would you choose multi-leader replication?
- What does R + W > N guarantee, and what still goes wrong?
- Last-write-wins: what data do you lose?
