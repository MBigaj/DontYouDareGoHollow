# Consensus and Leader Election

> **Why this matters for you:** Raft-style consensus underlies etcd, Kafka and every 'one leader only' guarantee.

## You should be able to
- Explain Raft at a whiteboard level
- Explain why consensus needs a majority
- Know where you use consensus indirectly

## Exam questions
- Why does a 5-node cluster tolerate 2 failures but not 3?
- How does Raft elect a leader?
- Where does your Kubernetes cluster rely on consensus?
