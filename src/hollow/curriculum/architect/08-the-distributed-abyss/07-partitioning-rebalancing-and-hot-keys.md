# Partitioning, Rebalancing and Hot Keys

> **Why this matters for you:** Splitting data is easy; keeping it balanced under real traffic is the hard part.

## You should be able to
- Compare hash and range partitioning, and consistent hashing
- Rebalance without downtime
- Find and fix hot keys

## Exam questions
- Why use consistent hashing?
- One celebrity user overloads a partition. Fix it.
- What happens during a rebalance to in-flight requests?
