# Failure Amplification

> **Why this matters for you:** Retry storms, thundering herds and cascading failures turn small problems into outages.

## You should be able to
- Explain retry storms, thundering herds and cascades
- Use retry budgets, jitter and request hedging carefully
- Recognise metastable failures

## Exam questions
- One dependency slows down and your whole platform falls over. How?
- What is a retry budget?
- Why can a system stay down after the original cause is gone?
