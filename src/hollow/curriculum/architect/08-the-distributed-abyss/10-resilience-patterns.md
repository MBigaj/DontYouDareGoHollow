# Resilience Patterns

> **Why this matters for you:** Timeouts, circuit breakers, bulkheads and load shedding are the senior toolkit for failure.

## You should be able to
- Apply circuit breakers, bulkheads and load shedding
- Combine them without making things worse
- Choose fail-open or fail-closed per feature

## Exam questions
- When does a circuit breaker make things worse?
- Design bulkheads for a service with one slow dependency.
- Load shedding: what do you drop first?
