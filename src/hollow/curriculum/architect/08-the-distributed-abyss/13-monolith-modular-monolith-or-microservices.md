# Monolith, Modular Monolith or Microservices

> **Why this matters for you:** The most common architecture debate; seniors decide with team, domain and cost, not fashion.

## You should be able to
- Compare the three styles on change cost, deployment and operations
- Decide when to split a service and when to merge
- Explain Conway's law in practice

## Exam questions
- Your team of 6 wants microservices. What do you ask first?
- What does a good modular monolith look like?
- When did splitting a service go wrong, and why?
