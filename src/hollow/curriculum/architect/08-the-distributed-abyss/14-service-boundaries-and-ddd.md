# Service Boundaries and DDD

> **Why this matters for you:** Wrong boundaries are the most expensive architecture mistake; bounded contexts help draw them.

## You should be able to
- Find bounded contexts and aggregates
- Draw boundaries around change and data ownership
- Spot a distributed monolith

## Exam questions
- What is a bounded context, and where are the ones in your platform?
- Two services always deploy together. What does that tell you?
- Who should own the 'user' data?
