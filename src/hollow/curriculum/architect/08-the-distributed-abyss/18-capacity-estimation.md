# Capacity Estimation

> **Why this matters for you:** Back-of-the-envelope numbers anchor every design fight.

## You should be able to
- Estimate QPS, storage, bandwidth and memory quickly
- Know latency numbers every programmer should know
- Turn estimates into design decisions

## Exam questions
- Estimate storage for 100M short URLs over 5 years.
- How many servers for 50k RPS at 20 ms each?
- Which estimate changed a design decision you made?
