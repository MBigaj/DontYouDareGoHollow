# Writing Decisions: ADRs and Trade-offs

> **Why this matters for you:** Seniors show their reasoning in writing; ADRs are how this game proves it.

## You should be able to
- Write ADRs with context, options, decision and consequences
- Explain trade-offs to engineers and to product people
- Decide under uncertainty and record what would change your mind

## Exam questions
- Write the ADR for one decision from your Lambda to ECS migration.
- Explain eventual consistency to a product manager in two sentences.
- What makes an ADR useful a year later?
