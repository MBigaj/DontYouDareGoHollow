# Recorded sources

The `.ogg` files here are from three sound packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), all released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use in
personal, educational and commercial projects, with no credit required. Credit is given anyway.

| Pack | Files |
| --- | --- |
| RPG Audio | `knife_slice*.ogg`, `draw_knife_3.ogg`, `handle_coins.ogg`, `footstep_*.ogg`, `book_place_1.ogg` |
| Impact Sounds | `impact_*.ogg` |

They came from the Godot asset-library ports of the packs (`Boyquotes/kenney-rpg-audio-for-godot` and `Boyquotes/kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot` on GitHub, both CC0-1.0), which carry the packs unchanged. Only the files `tools/sounds/sounds.py` uses are kept.
